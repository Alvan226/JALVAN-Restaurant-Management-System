JALVAN · DBT 1305

Restaurant Management System

JALVAN is a system concept designed to help track the main activities that take place in a restaurant, including menu management, customer orders, table reservations and customer enquiries.

Project purpose

Keeping restaurant activities organised

01

Menu activities

Present food and drink options with clear prices so customers can review what is available.

02

Orders and reservations

Support the recording and organisation of customer order details and table reservation requests.

03

Customer communication

Provide contact information and a clear way for customers to send enquiries to the restaurant.

Project scope

Restaurant activity tracking

The website demonstrates the main areas of a restaurant management system. The current version is a front-end prototype; saving records, processing orders and managing reservations require a connected backend and database.

Restaurant example

Savanna Table

Savanna Table is the example restaurant used to demonstrate the system. It represents a neighbourhood restaurant focused on good food, dependable service and a welcoming customer experience.
